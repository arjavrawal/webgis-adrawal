Developers for both front- and backend will be responsible for writing the code and managing the SQL/Python/etc (as necessary) execution of the website. The frontend interface designer will be responsible for providing QAQC and various identifying data sources.

## Working Agreement
We will meet every week on Wednesdays after class, beginning at 4:15, starting October 14 for the 4 weeks leading up to the next milestone/deadline. Meeting reminders and calendar invites have already been sent. To communicate, we will utilize an iMessage group chat. Commits to the repository are expected on an as-needed basis, once pages have been tested locally. We will also create a shared Google Drive folder to store meeting minutes.

## Decision Process
No decision will be made without a group consensus. We will fairly evaluate all benefits and tradeoffs; any ideas with major disagreements will be dropped from the scope of the project.

## Disappearing Member Clause
In case of emergencies, other members will address pending work. If a group member has an urgent commitment that prevents them from attending a meeting, virtual attendance may be a possibility, or the iMessage chat will be used to provide updates if necessary. If no response is received to messages within 12 hours, the professor will be contacted.
