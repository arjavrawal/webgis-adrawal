Shade is infrastructure.

Cool Corridors is a volunteer group in Tempe. Every summer we walk the routes people actually use between bus stops and cooling centers, and we record where the shade is and where it isn't.

Our maps go to the city's shade planners, to the cooling centers, and to anyone who has to make that walk at three in the afternoon in July.

This summer's routes

Library Loop

1.4 miles · 62% shaded

Tempe Transportation Center to Tempe Public Library along the Southern Avenue tree line.

Apache Line

1.1 miles · 38% shaded

Streetcar stops on Apache Boulevard to Escalante Community Center. The long gap is by the car lots.

Mill Avenue Walk

0.9 miles · 71% shaded

Tempe Beach Park to the downtown stops. Awnings do most of the work here.

McClintock Crossing

1.6 miles · 29% shaded

The McClintock and Southern stop to Kiwanis Recreation Center. Our hottest route and the one we most want fixed.
